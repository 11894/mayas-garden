Welcome in

A garden built for slow afternoons and big days

Tucked into the hills above Zomba, Maya's Garden is a working botanical garden first — hand-clipped hedges, stone paths laid one rock at a time, and a kitchen garden that keeps growing at the far end of the lawn.

It's also, quietly, one of the loveliest spots in the Southern Region to say "I do." Visitors and reviewers alike come back to the same word: spectacular. The kind of scenery that photographs itself, and grounds that reward a proper wander rather than a quick look round.

Open every day of the week, from ten in the morning until five in the afternoon — whether you're here to picture your wedding day, or simply to sit among the bougainvillea for an hour.

The bridal side

Weddings, engagements & the photographs you'll keep forever

"Happily ever after" isn't just a line here — someone's already chalked it onto the getaway bicycle.

The archway entrance, the stone stairway up to the house, the long lawns and hedge-lined paths — the whole garden was made for a ceremony, a first dance, or an afternoon of engagement photographs.

As a bridal services venue, Maya's Garden hosts the day itself and the pictures that come with it, set against grounds that need very little dressing up.

  • Ceremony & reception space within the garden grounds
  • A ready-made backdrop for bridal and engagement photography
  • Manicured lawns, archways and stone paths across the property
  • Open daily — enquire ahead to check availability for your date
